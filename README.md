# 📊 Amazon Sales Dashboard Analysis (Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Amazon Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard analyzes Amazon product data to uncover valuable business insights related to revenue, discounts, ratings, and product performance.

The dashboard enables users to explore sales trends and category performance through interactive visualizations and filters.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Electronics Generates Maximum Revenue
The Electronics category contributes the largest share of total revenue compared to all other categories.

### Smartphones Drive Sales
Smartphones & Basic Mobiles are among the highest revenue-generating products.

### Discounts Influence Customer Purchases
Products with medium-to-high discount ranges contribute significantly to overall sales.

### Strong Product Ratings
Most products maintain ratings above 4.0, indicating high customer satisfaction.

### Weak Correlation Between Price and Rating
Higher-priced products do not necessarily receive higher ratings.
